# Cover Letter Formula

Source: Splendor thread, five-paragraph structure.

1. **The Hook.** Genuine, specific enthusiasm for this company — something
   concrete (a product, a mission, a public challenge they're facing), never
   "I'm excited to apply for this role." Pull the concrete detail from
   `shared/company_research_cache/{company_slug}.md`
   (`12-company-research`) — if that cache found nothing beyond the JD
   itself, fall back to the JD's own stated mission/values language
   rather than inventing specificity. A generic-but-honest hook beats a
   fabricated-but-specific one.
2. **Technical Match.** Relevant skills in the JD's own terminology,
   keyword-rich but natural. This is where `04-keyword-analysis` output
   earns its place.
3. **The Story.** One brief, numbered example of relevant work — "built
   dashboards that reduced reporting time by 30%" beats "I have dashboard
   experience."
4. **Why This Role.** What specifically excites Kenechukwu about *this*
   position, referencing something from the JD, not a generic pitch that
   could apply anywhere.
5. **Close.** Confirm practical details (location/availability), express
   interest in discussing further, keep it short.

**Tone rules**: contractions are fine, sound like a human. Cut stock
phrases ("I am writing to express my interest in...") — recruiters have
read that sentence thousands of times and it signals a template, not a
person. Keep the whole letter under 400 words; respect the reader's time.
