## Leadership & Strategy

### Story: Redesigning Credit Assessment at Bank of Agriculture (2021)
- **Situation**: The credit reporting and loan application assessment model at Bank of Agriculture was outdated, leading to slow processing times and inconsistent risk evaluation.
- **Task**: Redesign the credit report process and the loan-application assessment model from scratch.
- **Action**: Redesigned the entire credit reporting workflow, rebuilt the loan assessment model using data-driven approaches, implemented automation where possible, and introduced new risk evaluation criteria aligned with modern agro-finance practices.
- **Result**: **Loan processing time reduced by 40%** (from ~7 days to ~4 days). Risk assessment accuracy improved, with 15% fewer default cases in the pilot quarter. The model was adopted across 3 branches before I left.
- **Skills demonstrated**: Strategic thinking, process design, financial analysis, Excel/VBA, change management
